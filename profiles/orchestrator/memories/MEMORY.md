Wiki-board review-routing gotcha: `kanban_request_changes` only works when the Reviewer run was claimed from `review` (`source_status: review`); claims from `ready` are rejected. Reviewer runs on dir-workspace wiki cards have repeatedly received an empty/non-Git `/workspace` instead of host `/srv/hermes/wiki`, even while Wiki Maintainer and Web Scraper receive the mount correctly. Triage auto-decomposition may rewrite/promote a card to `ready`, omit review provenance, and retry indefinitely; it can also generate separate stage cards that conflict with a required same-card review cycle.
